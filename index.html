Nana Yaw Ofori Gyasi, Esq.

As the founding partner of AKOBEN LEGAL SERVICES, Nana Yaw Ofori Gyasi has established a reputation for incisive legal analysis, formidable advocacy, and unwavering client commitment. The firm takes its name from the Adinkra symbol "Akoben" (the war horn), representing vigilance and readiness to defend our clients' rights at all times.

Navigating the complexities of the Ghanaian legal system requires not just knowledge of the law, but an understanding of the socio-economic landscape. We bridge that gap for local and international clients, corporations, and individuals alike.
