Counsel Profile

Meet the founder and learn about the ethos of Akoben Legal Services.

Nana Yaw Ofori Gyasie, Esq.

As the founding partner of AKOBEN LEGAL SERVICES, Nana Yaw Ofori Gyasie has established a reputation for incisive legal analysis, formidable advocacy, and unwavering client commitment. The firm takes its name from the Adinkra symbol "Akoben" (the war horn), representing vigilance and readiness to defend our clients' rights at all times.

Navigating the complexities of the Ghanaian legal system requires not just knowledge of the law, but an understanding of the socio-economic landscape. We bridge that gap for local and international clients, corporations, and individuals alike.

Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

At AKOBEN LEGAL SERVICES, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our clients and website visitors. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of Ghana.

2. Information Collection

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when scheduling a consultation, submitting an inquiry, or engaging our legal services. This may include your name, email address, phone number, and confidential details pertaining to your legal matters.

3. Use of Information

Your information is used strictly for the purpose of providing legal advice, responding to inquiries, and fulfilling our professional obligations. We maintain strict attorney-client privilege regarding all sensitive case information.

4. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission is entirely secure, and we advise caution when sharing highly sensitive information via unencrypted web forms.

Terms of Engagement

1. Scope of Representation

The submission of a form through this website does not constitute an attorney-client relationship. An attorney-client relationship with AKOBEN LEGAL SERVICES is only established upon the execution of a formal Letter of Engagement and the payment of any requisite retainer fees.

2. Consultation Fees

Initial consultations scheduled via this website may be subject to a consultation fee, which will be communicated to you by our clerks prior to the confirmation of your appointment slot.

3. Conflict of Interest

Before accepting any mandate, we are ethically bound to conduct a conflict of interest check. We reserve the right to decline representation if it conflicts with our duties to existing or former clients.

4. Client Obligations

Clients are expected to provide full, truthful, and timely disclosures of all facts relevant to their matters to enable us to provide effective legal representation.
