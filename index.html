Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

At AKOBEN LEGAL SERVICES, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our clients and website visitors. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of Ghana.

2. Information Collection

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when scheduling a consultation, submitting an inquiry, or engaging our legal services. This may include your name, email address, phone number, and confidential details pertaining to your legal matters.

3. Use of Information

Your information is used strictly for the purpose of providing legal advice, responding to inquiries, and fulfilling our professional obligations. We maintain strict attorney-client privilege regarding all sensitive case information.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement robust technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission is entirely secure, and we advise caution when sharing highly sensitive information via unencrypted web forms. Client data is retained only for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable laws and professional regulations.

5. Client Rights

Under the Data Protection Act, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by our firm. To exercise these rights, please contact our administrative desk.

Terms of Engagement

1. Scope of Representation

The submission of a form through this website does not constitute an attorney-client relationship. An attorney-client relationship with AKOBEN LEGAL SERVICES is only established upon the execution of a formal Letter of Engagement and the payment of any requisite retainer fees.

2. Consultation Fees

Initial consultations scheduled via this website may be subject to a consultation fee, which will be communicated to you by our clerks prior to the confirmation of your appointment slot. This fee may be credited toward your retainer should you formally engage our services.

3. Conflict of Interest

Before accepting any mandate, we are ethically bound to conduct a conflict of interest check. We reserve the right to decline representation if it conflicts with our duties to existing or former clients.

4. Client Obligations & Communication

Clients are expected to provide full, truthful, and timely disclosures of all facts relevant to their matters to enable us to provide effective legal representation. We commit to keeping clients reasonably informed about the status of their matters and promptly complying with reasonable requests for information.

5. Termination of Services

Either party may terminate the engagement subject to the terms outlined in the formal Letter of Engagement. Upon termination, the client remains responsible for payment of all fees and disbursements incurred up to the date of termination.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring to my initial consultation?

Please bring a valid form of identification (Ghana Card or Passport) and any documents relevant to your case. This includes contracts, correspondence, court documents, or property deeds. The more information you provide, the better we can assess your situation.

Do you charge for initial consultations?

Yes, we generally charge a nominal fee for formal legal consultations, as this involves providing preliminary legal advice and strategy. However, if you subsequently retain our firm for the matter discussed, the consultation fee is often credited toward your initial retainer.

How are your legal fees structured?

Our fees are structured based on the Scale of Fees provided by the Ghana Bar Association. Depending on the matter, we may bill on an hourly basis, offer a flat fixed fee (for specific services like incorporation or deed drafting), or work on a retainer basis for corporate clients.

Do you represent international clients?

Absolutely. We act as external counsel for several multinational corporations and foreign investors seeking to do business in Ghana, navigating them through local regulatory frameworks, the Ghana Investment Promotion Centre (GIPC) registrations, and local content laws.

AML & Regulatory Compliance

Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Policy

AKOBEN LEGAL SERVICES strictly adheres to the Anti-Money Laundering Act, 2020 (Act 1044) of Ghana and global best practices regarding financial compliance. As legal practitioners, we are designated as accountable institutions under the law.

Know Your Client (KYC) Requirements

Before accepting any mandate, particularly those involving financial transactions, real estate conveyancing, or corporate structuring, we are legally mandated to conduct thorough due diligence. Clients will be required to provide:

  • Valid government-issued identification (e.g., Ghana Card).
  • Proof of residential or registered business address.
  • Information regarding the source of funds for specific transactions.
  • Beneficial ownership declarations for corporate entities.

Anti-Bribery and Corruption

Our firm operates with absolute integrity. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy towards bribery and corruption. We do not engage in any improper payments or inducements to public officials, judicial officers, or private individuals to secure unfair advantages for our clients.
