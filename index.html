Counsel Profile

Meet the founder and learn about the ethos of Akoben Legal Services.

Nana Yaw Ofori Gyasi, Esq.

Nana Yaw Ofori Gyasi is a Ghanaian lawyer with experience in criminal law, civil litigation, dispute resolution, legal advisory and advocacy. His practice involves representing individuals, families, traditional authorities, businesses and corporate entities in a broad range of legal matters before the courts and other adjudicatory bodies in Ghana.

He has particular experience in criminal law, civil and commercial litigation, land and property disputes, chieftaincy and traditional authority matters, family and succession disputes, contractual claims, corporate and business-related matters, and employment and institutional disputes. He also advises clients on legal rights and obligations, risk management, contractual relationships and strategies for resolving disputes effectively.

Beyond courtroom advocacy, he places considerable emphasis on preventive legal advice and strategic representation, helping clients understand their legal position and take appropriate steps before disputes escalate.

Nana Gyasi is committed to providing legal services founded on professionalism, integrity, sound legal reasoning and practical results, with each matter approached with diligence and a clear understanding of the client's interests.

Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

At AKOBEN LEGAL SERVICES, maintaining the confidentiality and security of our clients' information is paramount. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal and sensitive data in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of the Republic of Ghana.

2. Information Collection

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when scheduling a consultation, filling out forms on our website, or during in-person meetings. This may include, but is not limited to, your name, contact details, identification documents, and specific details regarding your legal matters.

3. Use of Information

The information we collect is used strictly for the purpose of providing legal services, conflict checking, communicating with you regarding your case, and fulfilling our regulatory obligations. We do not use your information for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

4. Data Security & Confidentiality

All information shared with AKOBEN LEGAL SERVICES is subject to strict attorney-client privilege. We implement robust physical, technical, and administrative security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Disclosure of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Information may only be disclosed if required by law, by court order, or to third-party service providers (such as IT hosting) who are bound by strict confidentiality agreements to assist in our operations.

Terms of Engagement

1. Scope of Representation

The submission of a form through this website, or an initial consultation, does not constitute a formal attorney-client relationship. A formal relationship is only established upon the execution of a written Retainer Agreement signed by both the client and a representative of AKOBEN LEGAL SERVICES.

2. Consultation Fees

Initial consultations scheduled via this website may be subject to a consultation fee. You will be informed of any applicable fees prior to the confirmation of your appointment.

3. Client Responsibilities

Clients are expected to provide full, honest, and timely disclosure of all facts relevant to their legal matter. Failure to do so may impede our ability to provide effective representation and may result in the termination of the engagement.

4. Termination of Services

Either party may terminate the legal representation at any time, subject to the terms outlined in the formal Retainer Agreement and the ethical guidelines established by the General Legal Council of Ghana.

Legal Disclaimer

1. No Legal Advice Intended

The content provided on this website—including articles, practice area descriptions, and general information—is intended for informational purposes only. It does not constitute legal advice and should not be relied upon as such. The law is constantly evolving, and information on this site may not reflect the most current legal developments in Ghana.

Viewing this website, downloading information, or sending messages through our contact forms does not create an attorney-client relationship between you and AKOBEN LEGAL SERVICES or Nana Yaw Gyasi, Esq. 3. Liability

AKOBEN LEGAL SERVICES explicitly disclaims all liability in respect to actions taken or not taken based on any or all the contents of this site. Always seek the advice of competent legal counsel licensed to practice in Ghana for your specific legal issues.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring to my initial consultation?

Please bring a valid form of identification (such as your Ghana Card or Passport). Additionally, bring any documents relevant to your case, including contracts, court summons, correspondence, or land indentures. Having these documents ready allows us to provide a more accurate preliminary assessment of your situation.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

Yes, we recognize that our clients may be located outside of the Eastern Region or internationally. We offer secure virtual consultations via Zoom or Microsoft Teams. You can select "Virtual" as your preference when filling out the consultation booking form.

How does the firm charge for its services?

Our fee structures are transparent and comply with the Scale of Fees published by the Ghana Bar Association. Depending on the nature of the mandate, we may charge a flat fee, an hourly rate, or a retainer. We discuss and agree upon all fee structures with our clients during the initial consultation before any work commences.

AML & Regulatory Compliance

1. Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Policy

AKOBEN LEGAL SERVICES is strictly committed to combating money laundering and the financing of terrorism. As legal practitioners operating in Ghana, we are bound by the Anti-Money Laundering Act, 2020 (Act 1044) and the guidelines issued by the Financial Intelligence Centre (FIC).

2. Client Due Diligence (KYC)

We are legally obligated to conduct comprehensive "Know Your Customer" (KYC) procedures before taking on new clients or engaging in specific transactions (such as real estate purchases or corporate formations). Clients will be required to provide verified proof of identity, proof of address, and in certain cases, documentation verifying the source of their funds.

3. Reporting Obligations

While attorney-client privilege is fiercely protected, under Ghanaian law, the firm is obligated to report suspicious transactions to the relevant authorities if we have reasonable grounds to suspect that funds are the proceeds of illegal activities. We reserve the right to decline or terminate representation if a client refuses to comply with our AML requirements.
